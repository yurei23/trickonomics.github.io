About Trickonomics

Trickonomics makes short explainer videos about the pricing tricks and consumer psychology that stores use on everyday shoppers, and how to spot them.

About the app

Trickonomics Uploader lets the channel owner upload finished episodes to their own YouTube channel, along with each episode's title, description, and cover image, without doing every upload by hand.

The app signs in with Google and asks the channel owner for permission to upload to YouTube. It is not offered to the public, and it never accesses anyone else's account or channel.
