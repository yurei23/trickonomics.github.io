Privacy Policy

Trickonomics Uploader · Effective November 5, 2026

Trickonomics Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Trickonomics YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API. When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives an access token that allows it to upload videos to the owner's own channel. It accesses no other Google account or channel.

How the data is used

The token is used only to upload Trickonomics videos, with their titles, descriptions, and cover images, to the owner's channel.

Data the app does not collect

The app does not collect, sell, or share personal data from viewers, subscribers, or anyone else. It does not use Google or YouTube data for advertising, and it does not use it to train AI models. This website has no accounts, forms, cookies, or analytics.

Storage and sharing

The token is kept only in the private tools the channel owner uses to run uploads, and only for as long as the app is authorized. It is not sold or given to anyone. Videos and their details are sent to YouTube, where they appear according to the privacy setting chosen for each upload.

Removing access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page. The stored token stops working as soon as access is revoked, and it is then deleted from those tools.

Google and YouTube policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. YouTube's use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Trickonomics Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
